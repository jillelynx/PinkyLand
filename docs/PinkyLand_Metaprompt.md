# Metaprompt: "Pinky Land" — A Cozy Exploration Game

## 1. Your Role

You are an award-winning team of video game creators working together on one game. Speak and think as each expert when their skills are needed:

- **Creative Director** – protects the cozy, dreamy, heartfelt feeling of the whole game.
- **Game Designer** – movement, exploration, quests, rewards, pacing, and making it fun for kids and grown-ups.
- **Narrative Designer / Writer** – story, characters, gentle dialogue, journal entries.
- **Art Director & 3D Artist** – the dreamlike world, lighting, water, skies, characters, and creatures.
- **Animator** – lifelike movement for characters and animals, based on real animal reference.
- **Sound Designer & Composer** – soundscape, music, and little sound "rewards."
- **Technical Lead / Programmer** – builds a smooth, working game that runs beautifully on a Mac with an Apple M4 chip.
- **Child-Development & Accessibility Advisor** – makes sure the game is kind, safe, easy to learn, and never frustrating for young players.

Before writing any code, briefly introduce your plan as a team, then build the game step by step (see Section 14).

---

## 2. The Big Idea

**Pinky Land** is a cozy 3D exploration game about love, kindness, and imagination.

Pinky is a silky, pink toddler pillow with a cute smiley face drawn on him by a young child. His best friend on Earth is **Jacinta**, the girl who drew his face and dreamed up his whole world. Pinky wants to bring Jacinta to the imaginary world she created — **Pinky Land** — so they can explore it together.

The game should feel like a warm hug: no stress, no "game over," just wonder, discovery, friendship, and beautiful places to explore.

---

## 3. Inspiration (capture the *feeling* — do not copy art, characters, music, or levels)

- **A Short Hike** – relaxed open exploration, climbing and gliding, friendly characters who give little tasks, hidden secrets everywhere, a world you can explore in any order.
- **Abzû** – breathtaking underwater swimming, schools of fish, glowing reefs, the joy of gliding through water.
- **Journey** – wordless emotion, sweeping landscapes, glowing light, a sense of wonder and companionship.
- **Tchia** – playful freedom of movement, a cozy island world, campfires, music, and a strong sense of place.

## 4. Main Characters

**Pinky**
- A silky, **pale pink** toddler pillow that **stands upright, taller than it is wide (portrait, not landscape)**, with slightly rounded, gently pointed corners and a soft sheen.
- His face is **small and sits in the top third** of the pillow, drawn in **blue marker**: two chunky oval eyes and a **U-shaped smile** (two short sides and a rounded bottom), centered between and below the eyes.
- Sweet, brave, loyal, a little bouncy.
- Moves with soft, squishy, happy animations (squashes when he lands, flops when he sits).

**Jacinta**
- A young girl with **long, voluminous, dark-brown curly hair that falls well past her shoulders**, fair skin, dark brown eyes, defined dark eyebrows, and a gentle closed-mouth smile.
- Wears "dolphin" style athletic shorts and a cozy pink hoodie with little bear ears on the hood.
- When the weather is chilly (night, mountains, snowy areas), she automatically puts on leg warmers.
- Kind, curious, and brave. She created Pinky and Pinky Land.

**Liev**
- A young boy with fair skin and **medium-brown hair: fuller on top with bangs falling over his forehead, and short skin-faded sides** with a **lightning bolt design shaved into one side**.
- Wears sporty clothes: shorts and soccer jerseys.
- Energetic, loyal friend. He joins Jacinta in the Earth chapter to help with kindness quests, travels with her to Pinky Land, and is **a fully playable character**.

**The Pinkies**
- Pinky Land is home to many pinkies of all sizes — tiny pocket-sized pinkies, medium ones, and huge, gentle giant pinkies you can bounce on or nap on.
- Each has its own personality, color tint, and hand-drawn-looking face.
- They are Pinky's friends and help the player find secret items and paths.

---

## 5. Playable Characters

The player can choose to play as:

1. **Jacinta** – best at climbing, running, and talking with characters.
2. **Pinky** – lighter and bouncier; floats a little longer when jumping, squishes through small spaces.
3. **Jacinta carrying Pinky in her backpack** – Pinky peeks out of the backpack, reacts to what he sees, and gives hints or happy comments.
4. **Liev** – the fastest runner and highest jumper.

Characters the player is not controlling follow along as companions.

Switching should be quick and easy (for example, one key or button), and each choice should feel a little different to play.

---

## 6. Movement & Abilities

Everyone can:
- Walk and run
- Jump and double jump
- Bounce on fluffy clouds (like soft trampolines)
- Swim on the surface and dive to explore underwater freely (no stressful air meter — or a very generous, gentle one)
- Look around freely and zoom the camera in and out

Special abilities unlocked by exploring:
- **Wings** or a **special cape** – lets the player glide and fly. The cape is a shiny turquoise-blue satin cape (like Jacinta's real one).
- Other ideas for the team to suggest: faster swimming fins, a cloud-hop boost, a lantern that reveals hidden paths at night, a "Pinky hug" that calms scared animals, etc.

Movement should feel smooth, floaty, and joyful — never difficult or punishing.

## 7. Story Flow

### Intro
- Opens on a **child's crayon or marker drawing** of Jacinta and Pinky together in Pinky Land (sun, clouds, hills, water, hearts).
- The drawing gently comes to life and transitions into the game.

### Chapter 1 – Earth: Spreading Pinky Love
Jacinta begins in our world (a cozy neighborhood, park, school yard). To open the magic portal to Pinky Land, she must fill a **"Love Meter"** (or collect glowing hearts) by spreading kindness, such as:
- Standing up to a bully (calmly and kindly, getting help from a grown-up, being a good friend to the kid being picked on)
- Giving a bandage to a kid who got hurt
- Hugging someone who is sad
- Helping hurt animals (a bird with a hurt wing, a lost puppy, a turtle that needs help crossing the path)
- Other small kind acts the team can invent (sharing a snack, picking up litter, helping someone carry things)

Each kind act adds light to the portal. When it's full, the portal opens and Jacinta and Pinky travel to Pinky Land.

### Chapter 2 and beyond – Pinky Land
Free, open exploration. Gentle goals guide the player (for example, Pinky wants to show Jacinta his favorite places, or collect special "dream stars"), but players can explore in any order.

## 8. The World of Pinky Land

The world should feel like a dream: soft, glowing, peaceful, and beautiful.

**Sky & Weather**
- Big, fluffy, bouncy clouds
- Sunny days with soft light
- Gorgeous sunrises and sunsets with warm pinks, oranges, and purples
- Starry nights with twinkling stars, a glowing moon, and fireflies

**Water**
- Shimmering, crystal-clear water with sparkles and gentle reflections
- Ponds with lily pads and frogs
- Colorful coral reefs with fish, sea turtles, and other sea life
- Waterfalls with mist and rainbows

**Land**
- Rolling hills and flower-filled meadows
- Peaceful valleys
- Mountains in the distance (some can be explored later)
- Secret caves, hidden groves, and cozy nooks

**Day & Night Cycle**
- Timed like Minecraft (about 20 real minutes for a full day; the team may adjust).
- Different creatures, secrets, and activities appear at different times of day.

---

## 9. Cozy Camping

At night the player can:
- Sit by sparkling, crackling campfires (with warm glow and floating embers)
- Set up a tent and sleeping bag
- Sleep to skip to morning (with a sweet sunrise moment)
- Optional: roast marshmallows, look at constellations, hear a short bedtime thought from Pinky

---

## 10. Creatures & Characters Should Look Real (Not Just Shapes)

Animals and creatures must **not** be simple placeholder shapes like boxes, balls, or polygons. They should look like real animals with natural movement.

- Study real reference photos and videos of each animal (how a sea turtle paddles, how a deer walks, how fireflies blink, how fish school together) and use that to guide shapes, textures, and animation.
- Use high-quality, properly licensed 3D models and animations where possible (for example, free models labeled CC0 or "free to use," with credit given where required). Keep a credits list.
- Style: realistic shapes and movement with a soft, dreamy, slightly storybook glow so they fit the cozy world.
- Examples of creatures: butterflies, fireflies, bunnies, deer, foxes, owls, songbirds, frogs, dragonflies, fish, sea turtles, rays, dolphins, seahorses, jellyfish.

The people (Jacinta, Liev) should be appealing, kid-friendly 3D characters with smooth, natural animation.

---

## 11. Gameplay Features

- **Collecting:** shells, flowers, feathers, sea glass, dream stars, hidden Pinky drawings, and more.
- **Journal:** every item and creature discovered is added to Jacinta's journal with a little drawing and a short, sweet description, written as if by a child.
- **NPCs who help:** pinkies and creatures can be talked to; they give hints, little tasks, and lead the player to secret items and hidden paths.
- **Rewards:** new outfits, cape or wing colors, campfire styles, tent decorations, new abilities, and sweet celebration moments.
- **Special abilities:** unlocked by exploring and helping others (see Section 6).
- **Map:** a hand-drawn-style map that fills in as you explore, with markers for places you've found.
- **HINT button:** always available; gives gentle nudges (Pinky can give the hint in his own voice).
- **Camera:** look around freely, zoom in and out.
- **No losing:** no health bars or game over. If you fall, you float softly back to safety.

---

## 12. Sound & Music

- A soft, dreamy soundscape that changes with the place and time of day: birds and breezes by day, crickets and crackling fire at night, bubbles and muffled whale-like songs underwater, rushing waterfalls.
- Gentle, cozy music (piano, soft strings, chimes, light guitar) that swells during beautiful moments.
- Happy little sounds for collecting items, bouncing on clouds, opening the journal, and finding secrets.
- Separate volume controls for music, sound effects, and voices.

---

## 13. Technical Requirements

- **Must run smoothly on a Mac with an Apple M4 chip** (not a Chromebook), so the team can aim for rich graphics: beautiful water, lighting, shadows, glow, and particle effects.
- Recommended approach: a 3D game that runs in a web browser (Safari or Chrome) using a 3D web game toolkit such as Three.js, so it can be opened easily and shared online (for example, on GitHub Pages). If the team believes a different tool would produce a better game on a Mac, explain the trade-offs in plain language and recommend one before starting.
- Controls: keyboard and mouse, plus support for a game controller. Show controls on screen in a simple way.
- Settings for graphics quality, so it still runs well if needed.
- Save progress automatically.
- Organize files clearly, with labeled placeholders (especially for Pinky) that are easy to replace.

## 14. How to Build It (Work in Steps)

1. **Plan:** Summarize the game design, art style, and technical approach in plain, non-technical language. List any questions for me.
2. **Small first version:** Build one small, polished area first — the child's-drawing intro, a tiny piece of the Earth chapter, and one meadow with a pond in Pinky Land — with movement, camera, day/night cycle, and one campfire.
3. **Check in with me** before expanding.
4. **Expand:** Add the full Earth kindness chapter, then Pinky Land areas one at a time (meadows, ponds, reefs, waterfalls, hills, mountains).
5. **Add systems:** journal, map, hints, rewards, abilities, camping, NPC conversations.
6. **Polish:** lighting, water, creature animation, sound, and music.
7. **Test:** play-test each step, fix problems, and make sure it runs smoothly on a Mac M4.

At each step, explain what you made and how to open and play it, using simple, non-technical language (explain any technical word the first time you use it).
